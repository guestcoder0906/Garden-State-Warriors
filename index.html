Garden State Disabled Veterans Hockey Club Inc.

Our Mission

The Garden State Disabled Veterans Hockey Club is a veteran/player-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to empowering disabled veterans through the sport of hockey. Founded on the principles that this is the players’ team and every voice matters, we foster an environment of respect, accountability, trust, transparency and shared ownership. On the ice we compete. Off the ice we support. Together we continue our mission of service — to each other.

The organization was established in 2026 to provide therapeutic recreation, physical rehabilitation, mental wellness support, and community camaraderie to injured, ill, and disabled military veterans residing in New Jersey through the sport of amateur ice hockey. The organization operates to foster national and international amateur sports competition under the guidelines of USA Hockey's Warrior Hockey discipline, promoting holistic healing, stress management, and peer-to-peer transitional support for veterans dealing with physical injuries, PTSD, and other service-connected challenges.
